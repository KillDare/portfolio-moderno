About Me

I'm Kildare Alves, a Computer Engineering graduate and software developer from Brazil.

My journey in software development has taken me through different kinds of systems — from desktop applications and business software to mobile applications, APIs, and databases.

Working on real-world projects taught me something I value deeply: technology is most interesting when it solves a real problem.

I'm still building, still learning, and there's always another project waiting to be created.
